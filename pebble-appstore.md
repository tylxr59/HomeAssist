## Name

QuickAssist

## Description

Proposed copy:

Speak to Home Assistant from your Pebble Time 2. Open QuickAssist, speak your request, and read your assistant's reply on your wrist.

- Starts dictation immediately on launch.
- Uses your chosen Home Assistant voice assistant.
- Shows scrollable text replies and supports follow-up conversations.
- Connects to HA and loads your voice assistants directly in the phone settings page.

Requires Pebble Time 2, a compatible phone companion with working dictation, and a reachable Home Assistant instance with Assist configured. Pebble handles transcription; Home Assistant handles the request. Responses are text only. Commands are submitted immediately without transcription confirmation. Credentials are stored in the phone companion. Local URLs require phone access to your home network or VPN.
